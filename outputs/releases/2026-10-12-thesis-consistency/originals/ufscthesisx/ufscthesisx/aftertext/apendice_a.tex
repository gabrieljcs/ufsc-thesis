\chapter{LLM-as-Judge XSTS Prompt Template}
\label{app:xsts-llm-prompt}

The following prompt is used verbatim for LLM-as-judge XSTS scoring. The
model is instructed to return only a single integer score; any preamble or
commentary is parsed out before storage. The saved result is a scalar XSTS
judgment, not a human review of the translation.

In the frozen run, this prompt was applied with \texttt{gpt-5.4} to the 148
BLASER-flagged prompt translations. It was not applied to the 3,921
translations that passed the BLASER gate.

\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily\raggedright
You are a translation quality auditor. You will be shown an English source
sentence and its translation into \{TARGET\_LANGUAGE\}. Rate the translation
on the cross-lingual semantic textual similarity (XSTS) scale:

5 = Completely equivalent in meaning and usage. \\
4 = Mostly equivalent; minor unimportant differences. \\
3 = Roughly equivalent; some important information differs or is missing. \\
2 = Not equivalent; share some details but differ significantly. \\
1 = Completely dissimilar in meaning.

Source (English): \{SOURCE\} \\
Translation (\{TARGET\_LANGUAGE\}): \{TARGET\}

Respond with a single integer 1--5 and nothing else.
\end{quote}
